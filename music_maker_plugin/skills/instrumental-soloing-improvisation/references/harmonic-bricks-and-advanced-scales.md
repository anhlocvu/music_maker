# Harmonic Bricks, Substitutions & Advanced Chord-Scales

Move beyond single tonic scales by matching chord-scale colors to harmonic tension, blanketing functional bricks, and navigating standard turnaround and Rhythm Changes vocabularies.

## 1. Five Chord-Scale Families (Ordered Consonant -> Tense)

Select scales per chord function from consonant to maximally altered tension (synthesized from Reynolds' *Chord-Scale Syllabus*, Aebersold's *Red Book*, and Watkins' *Theory 2*). **Note**: Standard accidentals (`♭`, `♯`) are enforced throughout.

### A. Major Chord Family (`maj7`, `maj6`, `maj9`, `maj7♯11`, `maj7♯5`)
- **Ionian (Major)**: `1 2 3 (4) 5 6 7` — consonant tonic; treat natural `4` strictly as a brief passing tone (`m9` above `3`).
- **Major Pentatonic**: `1 2 3 5 6` — zero avoid notes; ideal consonant anchor and blanketing tool.
- **Lydian (`♯4`)**: `1 2 3 ♯4 5 6 7` — eliminates the natural-4th avoid note via `#11`, making all 7 degrees sustainable over `maj7`.
- **Bebop Major**: `1 2 3 4 5 ♯5 6 7` — adds chromatic `#5` (`♭6`) passing tone so chord tones (`1, 3, 5, 6`) remain on downbeats.
- **Lydian Augmented (`maj7♯5`)**: Melodic Minor Mode 3 (`1 2 3 ♯4 ♯5 6 7`) — shimmering modern tonic or `III` color.

### B. Dominant 7th & Sus4 Family (`7`, `9`, `13`, `7sus4`, `7♯11`, `7♭9`, `7alt`)
Following Aebersold's Dominant 7th Tree of choices (from clean function to outer tension):
- **Mixolydian**: `1 2 3 4 5 6 ♭7` — primary diatonic dominant (natural `4` acts as brief passing tone toward `3`).
- **Bebop Dominant**: `1 2 3 4 5 6 ♭7 7` — Mixolydian + `maj7` passing tone preserving downbeat chord tones.
- **Lydian Dominant (`7♯11`)**: Melodic Minor Mode 4 (`1 2 3 ♯4 5 6 ♭7`) — non-resolving/secondary dominants (`IV7`, `♭VII7` backdoor, tritone subs `♭II7`); removes natural-4 avoid tension.
- **Sus4 / Dorian `♭2`**:
  - Standard `7sus4`: Mixolydian or Major Pentatonic built on `♭7` / `5th`; quartal voicings and Phrygian colors.
  - **`sus7(♭9)`**: Melodic Minor Mode 2 (`1 ♭2 ♭3 4 5 6 ♭7`).
- **Whole-Tone (`7♯5` / `7♭5` with unaltered `9`)**: Symmetric hexatonic (`1 2 3 ♯4 ♯5 ♭7`; **only 2 distinct transpositions**). Supplies altered 5ths while preserving natural `9`.
- **Half-Whole Diminished Octatonic (`7♭9/♯9/13/♯11`)**: `H-W` alternating (`1 ♭2 ♯2 3 ♯4 5 6 ♭7`; **only 3 distinct transpositions**). Built from paired diminished 7th chords; highlights `♭9`, `♯9`, `♯11`, `nat13` over resolving dominants.
- **Phrygian Dominant (`7♭9♭13`)**: Harmonic Minor Mode 5 (`1 ♭2 3 4 5 ♭6 ♭7`) — classic minor-key `V7` sound; **Spanish Phrygian** adds `♯2`/`♭3` flavor.
- **Super Locrian / Altered / Diminished Whole-Tone (`7alt`)**: Melodic Minor Mode 7 (`1 ♭2 ♯2 3 ♭5 ♯5 ♭7` / `1 ♭9 ♯9 3 ♯11 ♭13 ♭7`) — maximum dominant tension (`♭9, ♯9, ♯11/♭5, ♭13/♯5`) before resolving to `I` or `i`.

### C. Minor Chord Family (`m7`, `m6`, `m9`, `m11`, `m(maj7)`)
- **Dorian**: `1 2 ♭3 4 5 6 ♭7` — primary jazz `ii7` and modal `i7` scale (bright `nat6`).
- **Aeolian (Natural Minor)**: `1 2 ♭3 4 5 ♭6 ♭7` — darker tonic minor (`♭6` tension).
- **Ascending Melodic Minor (`m(maj7)`)**: Mode 1 (`1 2 ♭3 4 5 6 7`) — stable tonic minor-major color (`6` & `maj7`).
- **Harmonic Minor**: `1 2 ♭3 4 5 ♭6 7` — minor-mode tension with `♯7 -> 1` and augmented 2nd (`♭6 - 7`).
- **Bebop Minor**: Dorian + `nat3` (`1 2 ♭3 3 4 5 6 ♭7`) — keeps `1, ♭3, 5, ♭7` aligned on downbeats.
- **Blues Scale**: `1 ♭3 4 ♯4 5 ♭7` — soulful `♯4`/`♭5` inflection across minor, dominant, and tonic major vamps.
- **Phrygian**: `1 ♭2 ♭3 4 5 ♭6 ♭7` — functions as dark minor color, dominant/`ii` substitute, or `i - ♭II - ♭III - ♭II` harmonic blanketing framework.

### D. Half-Diminished (`m7♭5` / `ø7`) Family
- **Locrian**: `1 ♭2 ♭3 4 ♭5 ♭6 ♭7` — parent minor `iiø7` mode (`♭2` is darker).
- **Locrian `♯2` (Melodic Minor Mode 6)**: `1 2 ♭3 4 ♭5 ♭6 ♭7` — raises `♭2` to `nat9` (`maj9` over `iiø`), eliminating harsh clash and smoothing `iiø -> V7` soloing.
- **Harmonic Minor Mode 2**: `1 ♭2 ♭3 4 ♭5 6 ♭7` — retains `nat6` color over `iiø7`.
- **Bebop Half-Diminished**: Locrian + `nat5` chromatic passing tone.

### E. Diminished (`dim7` / `o7`) Family
- **Whole-Half Diminished Octatonic (`W-H`)**: `1 2 ♭3 4 ♯4 ♯5 6 7` (**only 3 distinct transpositions**). Every note a whole step above each chord tone serves as an expressive non-harmonic extension (`9, 11, ♭13, maj7` over `o7`).

## 2. Modal Borrowing, Pentatonic Shortcuts & Triad Pairs (Mark Watkins)

- **Modal Borrowing Cadence**: Import **`iiø -> V7(♭9) -> I / i`** into major-key solos (especially bar before tonic arrival) for instant minor-inflected dramatic pull.
- **Chord-Specific Major Pentatonics (Tension Control)**:
  - Over **Major (`Cmaj7`)**: Play Major Pentatonic on **2nd degree (`D maj pent`: `D E F♯ A B`)** -> emphasizes `9, 3, ♯11, 13, 7` (Lydian color with zero root-heavy weight).
  - Over **Minor (`Cm7`)**: Play Major Pentatonic on **4th degree (`F maj pent`: `F G A C D`)** -> highlights `11, 5, 13, 1, 9` (Dorian sheen).
  - Over **Altered Dominant (`C7alt`)**: Play Major Pentatonic on **♭5 degree (`G♭ maj pent`: `G♭ A♭ B♭ D♭ E♭`)** -> projects `♭5/#11, ♭13, ♭7, ♭9, ♯9` with crystalline pentatonic intervallic structure!
- **Tonal Blanketing (`ii - V - I`)**: Blanket an entire `ii - V - I` cadence with the **Major Pentatonic built on the 5th degree of `I`** (e.g., `G maj pent` over `Dm7 - G7 - Cmaj7`).
- **Triad Pairs**: Alternate two adjacent triads lacking common tones to generate modern angular 4-bar/8-bar lines:
  - Over Minor / Dorian (`Cm`): `Cm + Dm`, or `E♭ + F`
  - Over Dominant (`C7`): **`D♭m + E♭m`** (delivers altered/Lydian-dominant color tensions via pure triadic shapes).

## 3. Harmonic Lego Bricks (John Elliott / Bob Keller Field Guide)

Decompose sprawling chord charts into recognizable modular progressions (**Bricks**) linked by **Joins**, **Approaches**, and **Substitutions**:

- **Brick Types & Core Cadences**:
  - **Four-Square**, **Passacaglia**, **Amen** (plagal color), **Compact / Long / Extended / Offset Cadence**, **GDS Cadence**, **Sad Cadence** (`iiø - V7♭9 - i`).
- **Modulating / Sequential Bricks (Indexed by Semitone Shift)**:
  - `Homer (0 st)` (stays in home key)
  - Ascending: `Cherokee (+1 st)`, `Woody (+2 st)`, `Highjump (+3 st)`, `Bauble (+4 st)`, `Bootstrap (+5 st)`, `Stella (+6 st)`
  - Descending: `Downwinder (-1 st)`, `New Horizon (-2 st)`, `Sidewinder (-3 st)`, `Half Nelson (-4 st)`
- **Standard Turnarounds & Joins**:
  - `Rhythm turnaround`, `Ladybird turnaround` (`I - ♭III7 - ♭VI - ♭II7`), `Foggy turnaround`, `Groovin' turnaround`, `Pennies Ending/Turnaround`, `Multi-subbed turnaround`, `Yardbird / Backdoor` (`iv7 - ♭VII7 -> I`), `Coltrane sub`, `Tritone / Dominant sub`, `Donna Lee opening`, `Doo Wop`, `Launcher (Slow/Quick)`, `Backslider`, `Dogleg`, `Hover`, `IV 'n Back`, `Night & Day`, `Nowhere`, `On-Off`, `POT` / `SPOT`, `Pullback`, `Rainbow`, `Rainy`, `Remote Off`, `Retake`, `Stablemates`, `Starlight`, `Straight`, `Super-Tension`, `Surprise`, `Tension ending`, `Two-Goes`.
- **Approach Chords (*There / Nearly-There / Further-Away*)**: Measure how far an approach chord sits from its target tonic to scale rhythmic/chromatic tension cleanly into the arrival chord.

## 4. Macro Improvisation Over Fast Changes (`Rhythm Changes` & `Blues`)

- **32-Bar `AABA` Rhythm Changes** (Mark Watkins Ch. 10):
  - **A-Section Options**: Original progression plus **Sets 1–6** harmonic/bassline substitutions (descending chromatic basses, `I-VI-ii-V` turnarounds, diminished passing chords).
  - **B-Section (Bridge) Options**: Classic `III7 - VI7 - II7 - V7` dominant cycle plus **Bridges 1–5** (`ii-V` pairs per dominant, tritone-substituted descending chromatic `ii-Vs`).
  - **Three Performance Strategies**:
    1. **Change Running**: Spell every `ii-V` and turnaround individually with guide tones and cells.
    2. **Blanketing**: Ride a single blues/pentatonic/tonic-major stream across fast A-section turnarounds (beats clutter at high tempos).
    3. **Hybrid Strategy**: Blanket tonic-prolongation bars (`1–4`), switch sharply into Change Running across the bridge (`III-VI-II-V`) and bar-7/8 turnarounds.
- **17 Blues Archetypes** (Dan Haerle / Red Book): Match scale and guide-tone density to the specific blues type—ranging from 3-chord basic blues through minor blues to chromatic **`Bird Blues`** (`ii-V` descending cycle in bars 1–4).
