# Beat Foundations & Pattern Architecture (Strudel / Ableton Learning Music)

*Distilled faithfully from `learning-music-production-with-strudel` (adapted from Ableton Learning Music & Strudel pattern curriculum).*

## 1. The Four-Layer Production Start
1. **Drums** — Define the rhythm, pulse, and groove foundation.
2. **Bass** — Locks rhythmically with the kick drum and outlines harmonic roots.
3. **Chords** — Establish harmonic color and spatial bed.
4. **Melody / Lead** — Carries the memorable motif on top.

## 2. Beat, Bars, and Tempo Ranges
- **Two meanings of "beat"**: (a) the drum pattern itself, and (b) the underlying steady pulse/tactus.
- **16-Step 4/4 Bar Grid**: Four beats per bar, subdivided into four 16th-note steps per beat (`1–16`).
  - **Beats 1, 2, 3, 4** correspond to steps **`1, 5, 9, 13`**.
  - **Four-on-the-floor kick**: Kick hits on steps `1, 5, 9, 13`.
  - **Standard Backbeat**: Snare or clap hits on beats 2 and 4 (steps **`5` and `13`**).
- **Multi-Bar Cycles**: Grooves typically span 2-bar or 4-bar phrases (adding turnaround hits or fills on the final bar before looping back to bar 1).

## 3. Core Pattern Archetypes & Sound Substitutions
- **Basic Rock Beat**: Kick on 1 & 3 (steps 1, 9), snare backbeat on 2 & 4 (steps 5, 13), steady 8th-note closed hi-hat (`1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15`).
- **Basic House Beat**: Kick on every beat (steps 1, 5, 9, 13), clap/snare on 2 & 4 (steps 5, 13), **open or offbeat hi-hat on the "&" offbeats** (`3, 7, 11, 15`), plus subtle syncopated percussion.
- **Role Swapping & Non-Kit Textures**:
  - Hi-hats are not mandatory to keep the 8th-note pulse: a clap, shaker, or rimshot can carry steady subdivisions while the snare hits sparsely (e.g., once per bar or displaced).
  - Body percussion (stomps + claps) or found percussion can substitute directly for kick + backbeat snare without changing the underlying rhythmic grid.
