# Pop Drumbeats, Syncopation, Hypermeter & Metrical Dissonance (Open Music Theory v2)

*Distilled faithfully from Open Music Theory v2 (`Rhythm and Meter in Pop Music`, `Hypermeter`, `Metrical Dissonance`, and `Drumbeats` by Bryn Hughes, Kris Shaffer, Megan Lavengood, Mark Gotham, and Scott Hanenberg).*

## 1. Straight Syncopation & Tresillo Mechanics
- **Straight Syncopation**: Take a chain of equal-value straight notes, **cut the first note in half**, and shift all subsequent attacks **early by half that metric duration**:
  - **Beat-level syncopation**: Quarter-note stream shifted early by an 8th note (attacks land on `"&"` offbeats).
  - **Division-level syncopation**: 8th-note stream shifted early by a 16th note (attacks land on `"e"` and `"a"`).
- **Tresillo (`3+3+2`)**: Approximates triplets inside duple subdivision (two dotted 8ths + one 8th inside a half note, or `3+3+2` sixteenths). Rotations `3+2+3` and `2+3+3` also occur.
- **Double Tresillo (`3+3+3+3+2+2`)**: Full-bar 16th-note expansion (e.g., guitar/synth riff against straight 4/4 kick and 8th-note hats).

## 2. Hypermeter & Metrical Dissonance
- **Hypermeter**: Strong/weak metrical grouping **above the barline** (most Western pop/classical groups bars in 2s, 4s, and 8s; grouping in 3s—*ritmo de tre battute*—or 5s/7s creates distinct asymmetric phrasing).
- **Metrical Dissonance** (two simultaneous ways of hearing the meter):
  1. **Displacement Dissonance**: Same meter/period, shifted in **phase** (e.g., a bass or clap accent shifted by a quarter note or eighth note against the main beat).
  2. **Grouping Dissonance**: Two conflicting internal groupings at once (e.g., **3-against-4** / `6/8` vs `2/4`, or `12/16` grouped in 3s resetting at the barline against a 4/4 kick). Used dynamically across sections to build rhythmic tension and resolve it back to straight pulse.

## 3. Drum Kit Anatomy & Groove Archetypes

### Kit Roles
- **Snare Drum**: Metal wires create broadband noise + sharp wood crack; anchors the **backbeat on beats 2 and 4** in quadruple meter (`4/4`), or **beat 3** in simple triple (`3/4`). Non-snare backbeats include claps, layered snare+clap, body percussion, or pedal hi-hat on 2 & 4 (jazz).
- **Kick Drum**: Lowest kit voice; more rhythmically flexible and syncopated than the snare, while still usually anchoring the downbeat of bar 1.
- **Hi-Hat / Ride / Crash**: Cymbals articulate steady pulses at beat (quarters), division (8ths), or subdivision (16ths) levels.
  - Closed hi-hat = crisp `"tick"`; open hi-hat = sustained rattle/accent; foot pedal = sharp `"chick"` backbeat; ride bell = bright cutting ping; crash = section downbeat punctuation or slow heavy pulse.
- **Toms**: Distinct pitched shells higher than kick; central to drum fills and tribal/tom-driven grooves.

### Standard Pop/Electronic Feel Variants (Relative to 80–160 BPM Tactus)
| Groove Archetype | Kick Placement | Backbeat / Snare | Cymbal / Pulse | Musical Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Basic Rock / Boom-Bap** | Beats 1 & 3 (plus syncopations) | Beats 2 & 4 | Steady 8ths (rock) or swung/16th hats (boom-bap) | Grounded, natural tactus (80–160 BPM) |
| **Four-on-the-Floor** | All 4 quarter beats (`1, 2, 3, 4`) | Beats 2 & 4 (clap/snare) or omitted | Offbeat 8th hats (disco/house) or 16th hats | Driving dance pulse (115–135 BPM) |
| **Half-Time Feel** | Beat 1 (and syncopated pickups) | **Beat 3 only** | Fast 16th hats / synth ostinatos keep double-speed surface | Heavy, spacious, suspended tension (trap, dubstep, breakdowns) |
| **Double-Time Feel** | Alternating twice per beat | **Every "&" offbeat** (e.g., `1 & 2 &` at ~300+ backbeat rate) | Fast 8ths/16ths | Frantic, urgent, punk/thrash/DnB momentum |
| **Dembow** | All 4 quarter beats | **Last two hits of `3+3+2` tresillo** (e.g., `"& of 2"` and `"4"`) | Minimal / shaker | Signature reggaeton & dancehall bounce |
| **Simple Triple (`3/4`)** | Beat 1 (plus tresillo/syncopations) | Delayed to **beat 3** | 8th or quarter pulse | Waltz-rock drive without alternating 2/4 symmetry |
| **Compound Quadruple (`12/8`)** | Beats 1 & 3 (dotted quarters) | Beats 2 & 4 (dotted quarters) | Triplet 8th divisions | Slow soul/R&B/blues ballad sway |
