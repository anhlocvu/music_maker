# Pop Ballad Arranging Navigation Map

Load **only the 1–3 files** needed for the current arrangement task, always in parallel with silent study of `music_maker_plugin/midi_map.md`:

| Task / Arrangement Layer | Reference File | Core Grounded Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Form, section arc, tension curve & ballad case studies | `references/form-tension-and-ballad-case-studies.md` | Verse-Chorus vs. AABA, *What Was I Made For?* & *K歌之王* blueprints, `0.618` global climax, `T1/T2/T3` timescale separation, ~30s variation rule, stealth layers & anti-repetition edits |
| Bassline design & low-end architecture | `references/bass-pop-ballad-craft.md` | Delayed bass entrance, slow legato phrasing, kick/hat lock, descending stepwise bass (`1-7-6-5-4-3-2-5`), chromatic pre-chorus, half-step approaches, pedal points, synth-bass/sub-bass shaping |
| Piano voicings, comping & sustain pedal | `references/piano-pop-ballad-voicings-and-pedal.md` | `3rd & 7th` basic chord sound, `C3–C6` comp range, no LH/RH doubling, `sus4/sus2` & `Bb/C` (`7sus4(9)`), available tensions, minimal finger voice leading, ballad CC64 pedal rules, non-collision with guitar |
| Acoustic & electric guitar patterns & interplay | `references/guitar-pop-ballad-patterns-and-interplay.md` | Never comping simultaneously with piano, upper-4-strings 3–4 note voicings, sharp-key open strings (`G, D, A, E`), substitute chord notation (`Em7` for `Cmaj9`), pick-to-bass attack lock, acoustic EQ/stereo clarity, minimal broken-chord color |
| String section orchestration & MIDI realism | `references/strings-pop-ballad-orchestration.md` | Studio section sizes (`5-3-2-2`, `7-5-4-4`, `8/6/4/4`, `2/2/2/2` octet), `2:3` balancing rule, omitting contrabass when rhythm bass plays, thinning keyboard chords, `3rd/7th` in cellos, half-open voicing, CC#11 expression, staccatissimo vs. legato, high violin descants, divisi tremolo & harmonics |
| Drum kit grooves, dynamics & realistic programming | `references/drums-and-percussion-pop-ballad.md` | Playing the form, 4-limb ergonomics, weeding & `1->2->4->8` Doubling Up, alternating L/R hat velocities, cross-stick/rim-shot vs. full snare, micro-timing drag/push, iterative quantize, room ambience & bleed, auxiliary percussion |
| Tutti hits, band kicks, setups & transitions | `references/tutti-kicks-setups-and-transitions.md` | Setup vs. kick/hit, `<= 1 beat` snare kicks vs. `> 1 beat` kick+crash kicks, on-the-beat kicks for off-beat entrances, unison/octave multi-octave tutti, percussive voicing rhythm-section lock, bell chords, variable density, stop breaks / dramatic pauses, and intro/coda/bridge transitions |
