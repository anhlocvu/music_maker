## Boundary Handoffs to Sibling Skills

- **Slow Pop Ballads** (formal `0.618` arcs, `T1/T2/T3` timescale separation, delayed legato vocals, `5-3-2-2` string scoring, tutti ballad kicks): activate **`pop-ballad-arranging`** alongside `references/pop-rock-and-contemporary-accompaniment.md` and follow its ballad rules.
- **Harmonic Function & Reharmonization Theory** (`II–V–I` substitutions, Neo-Riemannian `PLR`, Negative Harmony, Lydian Chromatic Concept, melody-to-chord scoring): defer tonal/modal chord selection to **`music-theory-harmony`** and **`melody-harmonization`**.
- **Full Band / Orchestral Layouts & Doublings**: coordinate section registers and horn-vs-rhythm roles via **`orchestration-arrangement`**.
- **Low-End Balance & Mix Separation**: hand sub-`100 Hz` kick/bass carving and stereo stem pan QA to **`bass-arranging-and-line-craft`** and **`mixing-timbre-mastering`**.
