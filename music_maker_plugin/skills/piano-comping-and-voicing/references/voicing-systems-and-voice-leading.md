> Grounded in Dr. J.B. ## 1. Acoustic Spacing, Register Hygiene & Omissions

1. **Harmonic Series Spacing (*Open Music Theory*)**: Mirror the natural overtone series—place **wide intervals (P8, P5, P4)** in the lower registers and **closer intervals (3rds, 2nds)** toward the top. Crowding thirds or seconds below `C3` creates acoustic mud.
2. **Ensemble Comping Window (`C3–C6`) (*Midwest Clinic — Dunscomb / Tolson; Aebersold Red Book*)**:
   - Keep active rhythm-section comping voicings roughly between **one octave below middle C (`C3`) and 1.5–2 octaves above middle C (`G5–C6`)**.
   - **Keep all ensemble comping notes at or above `C3`** when a bass player is active so the piano left hand never collides with the bass fundamental register.
   - *Solo Piano Override*: When playing unaccompanied solo passages (or reading Dan Haerle's treble-staffed root-included drills), transpose solo voicings down into warm middle-low piano territory while still honoring low-interval clarity.
3. **Rule of Omissions & Doublings**:
   - **Omit the Root** in LH or shared comping whenever a bass instrument supplies the root (*Dyas; OMT*). Restore the root at the bottom of the LH only in solo/bass-less textures.
   - **Omit the Perfect 5th First** when reducing chord tones (*OMT; Dyas*). Keep altered 5ths (`b5` on `m7b5`, `#5/b5` on `7alt`), because they define chord quality.
   - **Prioritize Essential Guide Tones (`3rd` & `7th`)**: The `3rd` and `7th` unambiguously declare major/minor/dominant quality and establish smooth horizontal voice leading (*Dyas; OMT; Pease & Pullig*).
   - **Avoid Doubling the Root Weakly** inside 4–6 part upper-structure or spread voicings unless separated properly or serving a unison/octave melody doubling (*Pease & Pullig Ch. 7*).
   - **Extensions Above the 7th**: Voice upper extensions above the chord's 7th (`13th` above `7th`, not tucked underneath where it reads as a `6th` chord) (*OMT*).

## 2. Smooth Horizontal Voice Leading & The `3–7` Paradigm

- **Lazy Voice Leading Priority (*OMT; Dyas*)**: Move every inner voice between adjacent chords using the smallest possible motion:
  1. Retain common tones in the exact same octave.
  2. Resolve active voices by half-step (`7th → 3rd`, e.g., `F → E` over `G7 → Cmaj7`).
  3. Move by whole-step.
  4. Leap only intentionally to reset a drifting comping register.
- **Two-Voice `3–7` Shell Alternating Rule (*OMT*)**:
  - Across **5th-related root motion (`II → V → I`)**, guide tones alternate cleanly between **`[3rd on bottom / 7th on top]` ↔ `[7th on bottom / 3rd on top]`** via half-step or common-tone resolution (`4ths`/`5ths` flipping smoothly).
  - Across **stepwise/2nd-related root motion (`II → III → IV`)**, guide tones track in **parallel** (`3→3`, `7→7`).
- **Three-Voice Extension Voice Leading (*OMT*)**: Place an upper tension (`9th` on `II-7`, sliding down by step to `13th` on `V7`, resolving to `9th` on `Imaj7`) on top of the `3–7` core so top voices sing a coherent stepwise line.

## 3. Rootless LH Voicings (Category A & B) & Two-Hand 5-Note Spreads

*(Dr. J.B. Dyas Mnemonics & Dan Haerle Core Hand Shapes)*

When a bassist anchors the roots, alternate **Category A** (`3rd` on bottom) and **Category B** (`7th` on bottom) across progression chains so consecutive voicings share almost identical pitches with minimal hand jumps:

| Chord Quality | Category A (Bottom → Top) | Category B (Bottom → Top) | Essential Identity / Avoid Tone Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Major 7 (`Cmaj7`)** | `3 - 5 - 7 - 9` (`E-G-B-D`) · 3-note: `3-7-9` | `7 - 9 - 3 - 5` (`B-D-E-G`) · 3-note: `7-3-5` | Ionian/Lydian; avoid `4th` (`F`) in tonal Ionian |
| **Dominant 7 (`C7`)** | `3 - 13 - b7 - 9` (`E-A-Bb-D`) · 3-note: `3-b7-9` | `b7 - 9 - 3 - 13` (`Bb-D-E-A`) · 3-note: `b7-3-6` | Mixolydian; avoid `4th` (`F`) unless `sus4` |
| **Minor 7 (`Cm7`)** | `b3 - 5 - b7 - 9` (`Eb-G-Bb-D`) · 3-note: `b3-b7-9` | `b7 - 9 - b3 - 5` (`Bb-D-Eb-G`) | Dorian (or Aeolian/Phrygian if modal) |
| **Half-Dim (`Cm7b5` / `Cø`)** | `b3 - b5 - b7 - R` (`Eb-Gb-Bb-C`) | `b7 - R - b3 - b5` (`Bb-C-Eb-Gb`) | Locrian `#2` adds `9` (`D`); includes `b5` & `R` |
| **Dom7 Altered (`C7alt`)** | `3 - #5 - b7 - b9` (or `#9` on top) | `b7 - b9 - 3 - #5` (`Bb-Db-E-G#`) | Altered scale; contains tritone + `#5` + `b9`/`#9` |
| **Dom7`b9` (`C7b9`)** | `3 - b7 - b9 - 5` (symmetric `dim7` on `3rd`) | `b7 - b9 - 3 - 5` | Tritone + `m3` + tritone symmetry |
| **Dom7`sus4` (`C7sus4`)** | Think `Gm7` Cat. A up a 5th (`b7 - 9 - 4 - 13(6)`, `Bb-D-F-A`) | Think `Gm7` Cat. B up a 5th (`4 - 13(6) - b7 - 9`, `F-A-Bb-D`) | `4th` replaces `3rd`; `3rd` is an avoid tone unless `T10` above `4` |

- **Two-Hand `II–V–I` Alternation Protocol (*Dyas*)**:
  - Path 1: `II` (Category A) → `V` (Category B) → `I` (Category A).
  - Path 2: `II` (Category B) → `V` (Category A) → `I` (Category B).
- **Two-Hand 5-Note Piano Voicings (*Dyas*)**:
  - Major: Cat A = `3 - 6 - 9 - 5 - R` (or `3 - 6 - 9 - 5 - 7`); Cat B = `7 - 3 - 6 - 9 - 5`.
  - Dominant `7sus4`: Cat A = `b7 - 4 - 6 - 9 - 5`; Cat B = `4 - b7 - 9 - 5 - R`.
  - Half-Diminished / Altered: split tritone and root/tensions evenly between LH (`5-4-2-1` / `5-3-2-1` grip) and RH (`1-2-3-5`) with zero mud below `C3`.

## 4. Available Tensions & Avoid-Note Rules (*Pease & Pullig Ch. 1–2*)

- **Standard Rule**: Scale tones lying a **whole step above** a chord tone are normally available tensions (`9`, `11`, `13`). Scale tones a **half step above** a chord tone are avoid notes (`S`-prefixed) creating harsh `b9` rubs against that chord tone—omit them from tonal voicings unless explicitly altering a chord or writing modal color:
  - **Tonal Major (`Imaj7`)**: Avoid `S4`. Keep `#11` (`T#11`) reserved mainly for endings/Lydian colors rather than routine mid-phrase `I` tonic unless specified.
  - **Tonal Minor (`II-7`, `III-7`, `VI-7`)**: In major keys, `III-7` and `VI-7` serve tonic function while `II-7` serves subdominant function; **`T13` (`♮6`)** is avoided on tonal `III-7/VI-7` and on tonal `II-7` (creates a tritone against `b3` that distorts subdominant function—usable only in modal Dorian); avoid **`Sb2`** (`b2`) and **`Sb6`** (`b6`) on tonal `III-7` Phrygian, and avoid **`Sb6`** on tonal `VI-7` Aeolian.
  - **Dominant Exceptions**: Half-step tensions **`Tb9`** and **`Tb13`** ARE usable on dominant 7th chords (supporting `V7 → I` or `V7 → i` tension). On `7sus4`, `4th` acts as chord tone and `3rd` is avoided except as a `10th` (`T10`) voiced clearly above the `4th`.
  - **Modal Exceptions**: Aeolian tonic allows characteristic `Tb13`; Phrygian tonic allows `Tb9` and `Tb13`.

## 5. Modern Jazz Voicings: Fourths, `"So What"`, Clusters & Upper Structures

### A. Quartal Voicings & `"So What"` (*Haerle; Pease & Pullig Ch. 5; Pamies Rodríguez*)
- **Diagonally Stacked Scale Fourths (*Haerle*)**: In Dorian/Lydian modal vamps, degrees `1, 2, 4, 5, 6` yield stacks of pure `P4`s; degree `3` places a tritone on the bottom; degree `7` places a tritone on top.
- **The 5-Note `"So What"` Voicing**: Bottom-to-top **`P4 + P4 + P4 + M3`** (`LH = bottom two P4s`, `RH = 2nd-inversion major triad on top`).
  - Build on degrees **`1, 2, 5`** of Dorian for `m7` chords (and `3, 6, 7` of Lydian for `maj7` chords); `D Dorian` `"So What"` grips also sound crisp over `G7sus` / `G7`.
- **5-Part & 4-Part Voicings in Fourths (*Pease & Pullig §5-1 to §5-3*)**:
  1. Voice downward from melody in `P4` or `aug4/dim5`; avoid adjacent 3rds in 5-part structures (in 4-part, one `M3` between top two voices is acceptable).
  2. Never double pitches—keep all 4 or 5 distinct so open quartal shimmer remains intact.
  3. Avoid `m9` intervals (except `+b9` on `Dom7b9` or modal characteristic color).
  4. Include the tritone inside all non-`sus4` dominant 7ths.
  5. Prefer quartal voicings on **sustained or percussive melodies**; sacrifice at most one 4th if essential to clarify `3` + `7`.

### B. Clusters (*Pease & Pullig Ch. 6*)
- Tight stacks of `M2` and `m2` intervals (`3–5` voices) for gritty percussive stabs or dense backgrounds.
- Avoid placing a `m2` between the top two voices; keep at least one `m2` inside inner voices for characteristic bite, and respect low-interval limits (`> C3`).

### C. Upper Structure Triads (UST) & Polychords (*Pease & Pullig Ch. 7; Haerle; UOML*)
- Project two audible identities simultaneously: a clear close-position upper triad (containing `>= 1` chord tension) floating over basic `3–7` / `1-3-7` support below.
- **Spacing & Resonance Rules (*Pease & Pullig §7-2, §7-4*)**:
  - Keep separation between the bottom of the UST and top of the lower support **between a 3rd and an octave** (wider splits sound disconnected; narrower masks the triad).
  - For maximal 4-part resonance, place a **2nd-inversion triad** supported a **Perfect 4th below**.
  - On `+7` (whole-tone), every degree supports an augmented UST; on `Dom7` symmetric diminished (half-whole), diminished/major USTs yield maximum dissonance (`b9, #9, #11, 13`).
  - Deploy USTs over **pedal points, phrase climaxes, endings (`maj7#11`), and percussive kicks**.
- **Proven Polychord Grips (*Haerle; UOML*)**:
  - **Oliver Nelson / Thad Jones 3-Voice Shell**: `#9 - 7 - 3` in middle register over root a 10th below.
  - **`9sus` Spread Grip**: Major triad built on `b7` over the root (e.g., `Ab / Bb` = `Bb9sus4`).
  - **Haerle Cycling Altered Dominants**: `1–7` or `1–3` LH shell + major triad a **major 6th above the root** in 2nd inversion.
  - **Haerle Polychordal `II–V–I`**: `3–7–9` in LH + parallel chromatic close triads in RH.
  - **Haerle Polychordal Blues**: `7–3–6` LH + 1st-inversion triad in RH.

## 6. Approach-Note Reharmonization & Ensemble Reductions (*Pease & Pullig Ch. 2–3; Absil*)

- **Target vs Approach Notes (*Pease & Pullig §2-3*)**:
  - **Target Notes** (long or accented): harmonize with full chord sound + available tensions.
  - **Approach Notes** (`<= 1 quarter note` moving stepwise: Passing, Auxiliary, Unprepared, Double Chromatic, Indirect `(S/ch)`): reharmonize undervoices via **Chromatic** (half-step slide matching melody direction), **Parallel** (exact semitone lock per voice), **Diatonic** (scalewise shift), or **Dominant** (`V7` of target chord), **OR keep lower voices steady/resting (`Independent Lead`)** for relaxed comping and pickups.
- **Big Band / Mixed Mechanical Reductions (*Pease & Pullig Ch. 3; Absil §3.4.3*)**:
  - Translate horn-section **4-Way Close**, **Drop 2** (re-voice cleanly at diminished passing chords to avoid awkward jumps — *Absil*), **Drop 3**, **Drop 2+4** (substitute `13` for `5` or `9` for `1`), and **5-Part Spreads** (bottom = Root, inner = `3` & `7` guide tones, upper-mid = tension/chord tone, top = lead) onto two hands when doubling or covering full-band tutti kicks.
