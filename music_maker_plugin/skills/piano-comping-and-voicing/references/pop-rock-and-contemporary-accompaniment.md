# Pop, Rock & Contemporary Keyboard Accompaniment

> Grounded in Michael Gundlach (*Pop Piano Method*), Ron Drotos (*Pop Ballad Accompaniment for Piano and Keyboard*), James Michael Stevens (*Contemporary Accompaniment Patterns*), Robert Hutchinson (*Music Theory for the 21st-Century Classroom*), and *Open Music Theory* (*Pop-Rock Texture*).

---

## 1. Foundational Pop/Rock Role Division (*Gundlach's 3 Rules; OMT*)

1. **Five-Layer Functional Placement (*OMT Pop-Rock Texture*)**: Place piano cleanly inside **Harmonic Filler** (or **Melodic** when carrying an instrumental hook/solo—never stepping on lead vocals). When a bass guitar or synth bass is present, leave **Functional Bass** alone so the sub-`C3` octave stays clear.
2. **Rule 1 — LH Adaptation (Solo vs Band)**:
   - **Without a Bassist**: LH takes the band bassist's role—anchor the **chord Root on beat 1**, alternating with **Root–5th–Octave** shapes (`1–5–8`, neutral regarding major/minor so RH defines chord color without mud).
   - **With a Bassist Active**: Lift LH up out of bass territory (`>= C3`), playing lighter 5ths, sustained pads, or alternating broken fills above the bass groove.
3. **Rule 2 — Melody-Under-Chord Voicing**: When piano simultaneously plays a song melody and accompaniment, keep the **melody strictly in the highest RH voice** and hang supporting triad/7th chord tones underneath on stressed beats (**`1 & 3` in 4/4**, **`1` in 3/4**).
4. **Classical Keyboard vs Chorale Spacing Discipline (*Hutchinson*)**:
   - Keep gaps between **Soprano–Alto** and **Alto–Tenor** strictly **`< 1 octave`** (`<= 12` semitones); **Bass–Tenor** may open wider (`> 1 octave`).
   - On 7th chords, resolve the 7th downward by step; in circle-of-fifths sequences, alternate complete and incomplete 7th chords smoothly.
   - Over descending chromatic basslines (`1–7–b7–6–b6–5`), move RH triads/inversions by contrary or oblique motion.

## 2. Gundlach's 6 RH Variations (Replacing Square Block Chords)

Avoid monotonous root-position block banging by cycling through these six RH textures across sections:

1. **Inversions for Smooth Voice Leading**: Connect progressions via common tones within a tight `C4–E5` window instead of root-position hand jumps.
2. **Omit the 3rd (Open Power / Fifths)**: Play `1–5–8` for muscular Folk, Indie, Pop-Rock, or Anthem choruses where clarity matters more than warm 3rds.
3. **Omit 3rd + Add 9th/2nd (`add2 / sus2`)**: Replace the 3rd with the `2nd/9th` for airy, shimmer-rich contemporary singer-songwriter and CCM textures.
4. **Vary 3rd with 4th (`sus4 ↔ 3`)**: Suspend the 4th on strong beats and resolve to the 3rd (or linger unresolved on upbeat shifts) to build gentle harmonic yearning.
5. **Split-Up Chords & Broken Oscillations**: Alternate the bottom thumb note against the top two chord notes (`1 ↔ 3+5` or `1 ↔ 2+5`) at 8th-note or 16th-note subdivisions.
6. **Embellishing / Hybrid Slash Chords**: Embellish a major chord with a minor triad a **whole step above** (e.g., `Dm` triad in RH over `C` bass on `C major`), and embellish a minor chord with a major triad a **whole step below** (e.g., `C major` triad in RH over `D` bass on `D minor`, giving `Dm11(no3)` / `C/D` color).

## 3. Pop/Rock Idioms, Countermelody & Guitar Translation (*Drotos; Stevens*)

- **Quarter-Note Drive (*Elton John / Paul McCartney*)**:
  - Pulse crisp, medium-heavy close triad inversions on **all four quarter beats (`1-2-3-4`)** in RH while LH locks steady **roots or `root–octave` eighth/quarter rock pushes** underneath. Works cleanly for upbeat pop, piano-rock, and driving choruses.
- **Sustained Top-Voice Countermelody + Inner Pulse (*Drotos*)**:
  - Hold the uppermost RH chord tone (fingers `4–5`) for half or whole bars to sing an independent upper line while fingers `1–3` gently pulse steady 8th notes on inner tones (`1–3` / `5–1`).
- **Acoustic-Guitar Arpeggio Imitation (*Drotos*)**:
  - When a track has no acoustic guitar, translate fingerpicked/flatpicked guitar patterns onto the keyboard using wide smooth 8th- or 16th-note cross-hand arcs (`Root – 5 – 8 – 9/3 – 5`).
- **Slash Chords for Stepwise Bass Connection (*Drotos*)**:
  - Replace static root chords with **1st/2nd inversions (`C/E`, `C/G`)** or **`IV/V` hybrids (`F/G`, `Dm7/G`)** so bass motion flows chromatically or stepwise beneath stable RH inversions.

## 4. Progressive Pattern Palette & Pattern 24 Subtraction (*Stevens*)

Scale rhythmic activity across Verse → Pre-Chorus → Chorus using Stevens' accompaniment concepts:

| Section Energy | Pattern Device (*Stevens*) | Execution Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Intimate Intro / Verse 1** | **Pattern 24: "No Piano" (Silence)** | **Rest completely for 2–8 bars**, letting guitar, pad, or stripped bass+drums carry the groove so piano's later entrance has emotional impact. |
| **Sparse Verse** | **Half-Note Sustain + Beats 3–4 Rest** | Strike a spread `add2`/`sus2` chord on beat 1 (`half note`), then **rest beats 3–4** to leave space for vocal phrasing. |
| **Flowing Verse / Bridge** | **Broken Wave + Half-Bar Inversion** | Sweep bottom-up or top-down on beats `1–2`, then **shift to the next inversion on beats `3–4`** for subtle melodic shimmer. |
| **Building Pre-Chorus** | **Alternating Block & Broken + Syncopated Open** | Accent beat 1 as a block chord, then break beats `2–4`; on syncopated pushes (`& of 2`, `& of 4`), **omit the middle chord tone** (`root + 5/9/7`) for crisp acoustic ring. |
| **Full Piano Solo Feature** | **LH Open `1–5–8–9–10` Roll** | Sweep wide 10ths in LH against lyrical RH inversions when no low bass guitar competes below `C3`. |
| **Syncopated Pop Groove** | **LH Bass-Guitar Mimic** | Echo electric bass anticipation rhythms in LH (`C3+`) while RH plays compact upper-register chordal stabs. |

## 5. Sustain Pedal (`CC64`) Clarity Standard (*Gundlach; Drotos*)

- **Understand Full-Bar vs Per-Chord Sustain**: *Gundlach* notes that holding damper pedal across a complete bar creates a flatter **"two-dimensional" sound** useful selectively on static/dreamy pop-ballad textures, whereas moving chord progressions, Rock grooves, and active basslines require **per-chord pedal refreshes** (*Drotos*) so tones stay distinct without mud.
- **Per-Chord / Half-Pedal Protocol**:
  - Refresh `CC64` cleanly **per chord change** (or on every beat/half-bar during busy passing basslines or driving quarter-note rock).
  - Release slightly right at (or microseconds after) the new chord attack (`half/flutter pedal` feel) so resonances bloom without blurring adjacent bass roots (`E1–B2`).
- **Strumming/Rolling Expressive Voicings**: Use subtle bottom-up or top-down micro-rolling on lush ballad and acoustic-imitation chords rather than rigid robotic block attacks.
