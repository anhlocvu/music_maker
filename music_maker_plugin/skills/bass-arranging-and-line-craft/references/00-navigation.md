## Boundary Handoffs to Sibling Skills

- **Slow Pop Ballads** (delayed legato attacks, descending slash-chord basslines walking down progressions, restrained low-register ballad support): activate **`pop-ballad-arranging`** first, pairing with `references/kick-bass-lock-and-low-end-clarity.md`.
- **Fretboard Mechanics, Slap/Pop/Tap & Pitch Bends** (left/right hand string mechanics, per-string MIDI/MPE bend isolation, vibrato curves, slides, fretless scoops): delegate physical articulation modeling to **`guitar-bass-expression`**.
- **Full Drum Kit Pocket & Rhythm-Section Grooves**: pair kick patterns and ride-cymbal swing timing via **`groove-rhythm-drums`**.
- **Piano Register Non-Collision (`>= C3`) & Root Omission**: coordinate keyboard LH clearance with **`piano-comping-and-voicing`**.
- **Final Mix Balance, LUFS & Stereo Stem QA**: verify overall spectral balance and centered low-end stems with **`mixing-timbre-mastering`**.
